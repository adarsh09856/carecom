Our Heritage & Commitment

Operating from Olakha, the heart of Bhutan's automotive district

Thimphu Auto Care was founded with a single mission: to provide vehicle owners across Bhutan with transparent, dealership-level mechanical precision without exorbitant costs. Navigating the winding mountain highways of Bhutan requires supreme vehicle reliability — from Dochula Pass in heavy snowfall to the rough stretches of the East-West Highway.

Our state-of-the-art facility features 6 hydraulic service bays, computerised 3D wheel aligners, factory-level OBD-II electronic scan tools, and a dedicated dust-free paint booth. We are also recognized as an authorized cashless accident repair center for Royal Insurance Corporation of Bhutan (RICB) and Bhutan Insurance Limited (BIL).

  • 🛡️ Trusted by 12,000+ local Bhutanese families, taxi fleets and government bodies
  • ⚙️ 100% genuine factory parts sourced direct from Japan, Korea and India
  • 📍 Central facility in Olakha with 24/7 mountain breakdown recovery service
